Imagine This Happening Automatically.

Practical workflows your team can launch, monitor, and scale with confidence.

New Lead

  1. Website or form lead arrives.
  2. AI qualifies source and intent.
  3. CRM profile is created with context.
  4. WhatsApp intro message is sent instantly.
  5. Sales owner gets priority notification.

Customer Inquiry

  1. Question comes from WhatsApp or site chat.
  2. AI answers based on approved business rules.
  3. Complex requests are escalated to human team.
  4. Conversation and tags sync to CRM.
  5. Follow-up task is scheduled automatically.

Appointment

  1. Booking request is captured.
  2. AI checks available calendars and constraints.
  3. Customer receives available slot options.
  4. Confirmation + reminders are sent.
  5. CRM event and team timeline are updated.

Sales Follow-Up

  1. Proposal is sent to a qualified lead.
  2. Response window is tracked automatically.
  3. Timed email + WhatsApp follow-up is triggered.
  4. Owner task is created for personal outreach.
  5. Outcome is logged for optimization insights.
